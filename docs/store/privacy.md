# Scenecast Recorder: privacy

Scenecast Recorder captures the pages of a web demo while you record it in the Scenecast desktop app: each screen's
markup, styles, fonts and a picture of it. It sends them only to the Scenecast app on the same computer
(127.0.0.1), and only while you've started a recording there.

It sends nothing to anyone else, keeps no analytics, and doesn't sell or share data. What it records stays on your
computer, in the folders Scenecast shows you; deleting a demo in Scenecast moves its recording to the Trash (Recycle
Bin on Windows).
