Scenecast 0.5.5: recordings without scroll bars or leftover outlines, and fewer surprises while you work.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.5.5** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's fixed**

- **No scroll bar in your screens' pictures** on a Mac that always shows scroll bars (with a mouse connected, say). Review no longer outlines one as a spot.
- Finishing a step (a build, checks, the talk track) no longer shows it as done before its result is in.

**Known issues**

- If Scenecast's window ever goes blank, quit it (⌘Q) and open it again; nothing is lost.
