Scenecast 0.4.0: you don't need to know the beats. GitHub Copilot records, scripts or coaches the demo with you.

**Update:** click **Update** at the bottom left of Scenecast (or download the installer below). Your demos stay as they are.

| | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) | `Scenecast_0.4.0_universal.dmg` |
| Windows | `Scenecast_0.4.0_x64-setup.exe` (no admin), or `Scenecast_0.4.0_x64_en-US.msi` |

**What's new**

- **New demo asks how you want to make it:**
  - **Record for me.** Say what the demo should show (one line is enough), or give it your notes, a doc, an email or a
    script (a RunLines `.sk` too). Copilot drafts a plan from that and each app's start screen: the beats, what to
    click and type, what to say. Read it, change anything, **Approve and record**. Copilot then clicks through in your
    own signed-in browser while the recorder captures everything, and waits for AI answers to finish.
  - **I'll click, you script it.** Click through once without knowing the beats; Copilot writes the beats and what to
    say afterwards.
  - **Coach me.** Click through yourself while Copilot shows the next step and what to say, here and in a small coach
    window for your laptop screen.
  - **I know my demo.** As before.
- **Then it finishes the demo for you:** the talk track from what actually happened, the build, the focus points, the
  presenter guide, and the checks for them. Edit the script under **Script** like any other.
- **Nothing is sent, saved or changed without you.** Any step that could send, post, approve, buy, delete or change a
  setting stops and asks **May I?**: Do it, I'll click it myself, Skip it, or Stop. When a screen isn't what it
  expected, it works out what to do or asks you.
- **GitHub Copilot through your own GitHub account.** On the new **Copilot** page, **Sign in with GitHub** and enter
  the code it shows. No keys. The first sign-in gets Copilot ready on your computer (about 80 MB, once).
- **You see what Copilot sees.** Only the goal, the plan and the screen at hand (its buttons and boxes by name, and its
  words unless you turn that off on the Copilot page); email addresses, IDs and tokens are taken out first; never
  screenshots. Every request is listed on the demo's Record step.

**What you do:** click **Update**, then open the **Copilot** page and **Sign in with GitHub**. Details:
[docs/ai.md](https://github.com/jldeen/scenecast/blob/main/docs/ai.md).
