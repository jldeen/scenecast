Scenecast 0.4.7: Record works on real Microsoft 365 tabs, and you see when there's a new version.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.4.7** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's fixed**

- **Record starts in seconds.** Some frames inside Teams, Copilot and the admin center never answer. Record used to wait on them, sat on "Starting…" for minutes, and then said "timeout waiting for …". Now it waits 5 seconds, leaves that frame out and says so in the log.
- **Prompts work end to end.** Type a prompt and press Enter while recording: the real answer is captured as it streams in, and in the replica the same prompt and Enter replay it.
- **Prompts and clicks are never stuck.** If one frame inside a Microsoft app stopped answering, the recorder stopped taking your clicks and Enter (they were held and never done). It now leaves that frame out after 5 seconds, and nothing the recorder asks the browser can wait for ever.
- **Less waiting after each screen.** The automatic capture of a screen's hovers, menus and zoom levels ran 6 to 13 minutes on real apps. It now stops at 90 seconds per screen, and zoom stops at that budget too (it took 7 minutes on Microsoft IQ).
- **Demos with Microsoft IQ build.** Power BI keeps a sign-in token in the page, and the build rightly refused to go on with a credential in the captures. The recorder now leaves token attributes out when it captures a screen.
- **Record again, then Build, builds the new take.** It used to build the first recording again.
- **The recorder stays connected while it saves a big screen.** Scenecast no longer restarts its receiver because of one slow answer.
- **Record finds the tabs Check just found.** If the recorder was connected from more than one browser (another browser or profile with the extension, or a test browser), Check could open the apps' tabs in one and Record look for them in another. That gave "not open: teams …" right after "Ready to record". Scenecast now records in one browser only, and Check names the problem when the recorder is in more than one.
- **Your browser's profiles show** on New demo and the Record step. They stayed on "Looking at the browser's profiles…" when your profiles have pictures.
- No more empty coloured boxes in the sidebar and on New demo.
- The four ways to make a demo are real buttons, for the keyboard and screen readers.

**What's new**

- **The guide says where to type and click.** Beats have plain names ("Copilot: after “Summarize…”", not "copilot-03-summarize-the-supp"). Until you add a script, the presenter guide (**⌥G**, on your laptop screen, never on the stage) lists each beat's steps from the recording: "Type `…` (**⌥P** types it)", "Press **Enter**: the answer streams in", "Click **Library**".

- **A new version shows by itself.** Scenecast checks when it starts, every 30 minutes, and when you come back to its window. A red dot appears on **Check for updates**, with **Update to vX.Y.Z**. Click it to install and restart. Nothing installs on its own, and the button waits while you're recording or presenting.
