Scenecast 0.4.10: Record for me finishes every step, each take starts clean, and the app keeps up while your browser is in front.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.4.10** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's fixed**

- **Record for me's last click is recorded.** The recorder holds each click until it has captured the screen before it, then does it. On busy apps (the admin center has 10 frames) that takes a second or more, and Copilot moved on as soon as the recorder looked idle. The last click was dropped when it stopped, so "Click All agents" never happened. Copilot now waits until the recorder has done each click, Enter and typing.
- **"May I?" shows while your browser is in front.** While Copilot clicked in your browser, Scenecast's window stayed on "Starting…", and its questions only showed once you clicked its window. A Mac pauses drawing in a covered window; Scenecast now updates anyway.
- **"Recorder connected" is right.** The Record step could say "Recorder not connected" while it was connected, after the browser had been in front.
- **Each take starts clean.** Clicks you made between takes (tidying up your tabs after Stop) were kept by the page and replayed at the start of the next recording. A new recording now starts with none.
- **Check and fix puts each app back on its start page.** A second take used to start wherever the last one left a tab (in Copilot, the last take's chat, which would show on stage). Check and fix now loads the app's address again when a tab is elsewhere in the app.
