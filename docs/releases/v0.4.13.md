Scenecast 0.4.13: Record for me finishes every step, each take starts clean, and the app keeps up while your browser is in front.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.4.13** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's fixed**

- **Record for me's last click is recorded.** A page sometimes holds a click for the recorder to do a moment later. Stop came first and dropped it, so "Click All agents" never happened and its screen was missing. Stop now finishes what the page is holding first, for you and for Copilot, and Copilot waits until the recorder has picked up each click, Enter and typing.
- **Typing replaces what's in the box.** Copilot puts your last unsent prompt back in its box, and Record for me typed on top of it, so the prompt came out twice. It now empties the box first.
- **"Do it" takes the first click.** The "May I?" buttons were rebuilt each time the line above them counted a screen, so a click could miss. They now stay put.
- **"May I?" shows while your browser is in front.** While Copilot clicked in your browser, Scenecast's window stayed on "Starting…", and its questions only showed once you clicked its window. A Mac pauses drawing in a covered window; Scenecast now updates anyway.
- **"Recorder connected" is right.** The Record step could say "Recorder not connected" while it was connected, after the browser had been in front.
- **Record again, then Build, builds.** Focus points from the last talk track could name a beat the new take doesn't have, and the build stopped. They're now left out until Script makes new ones. When a build does stop, Scenecast says why in plain words and writes it to its log.
- **Each take starts clean.** Clicks you made between takes (tidying up your tabs after Stop) were kept by the page and replayed at the start of the next recording. A new recording now starts with none.
- **Check and fix puts each app back on its start page.** A second take used to start wherever the last one left a tab (in Copilot, the last take's chat, which would show on stage). Check and fix now loads the app's address again when a tab is elsewhere in the app.
