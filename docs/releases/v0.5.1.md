Scenecast 0.5.1: Scenecast says where a recording's time goes, the first step to making it much faster.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.5.1** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's new**

- **Review shows where the time went:** "Recorded in 21 min · finished in 4 s · built in 1 min 10 s".
- **The recording keeps a timeline:** each app's start, each screen's menus and hovers by kind, every click and answer, how long your clicks waited for Ready, and your own time. Details in docs/engine/speed.md.
- **A benchmark:** `scenecast ai bench` runs a saved plan with Record for me, unattended, and times every phase.

**What's fixed**

- The recorder's "fixed waits" figure was too high: timeouts and loops running side by side were counted, some twice.
