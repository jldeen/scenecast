Scenecast 0.5.2: ready to present about a minute after Stop, and less waiting before and during a recording.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.5.2** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's new**

- **Ready to present soon after Stop:** the replica is built from what was already scanned while you recorded, and Present doesn't wait for the checks and the comparison with the real product; they run in the background. A big demo went from about 1½ minutes to about 20 seconds; building again with nothing changed takes a few seconds.
- **Check and fix looks at every app at once,** and Record doesn't repeat it if you ran it in the last 10 minutes.
- **Recorded light, even on a dark Mac:** a tab that shows dark is shown light for the recording, that tab only. Set the demo's theme to dark to keep it dark.
- **Shorter waits for menus and hovers:** Scenecast learns how quickly each app shows a hover and waits only that long, and stops trying kinds of controls that never show anything.
- **Zoom:** a zoom level that looks the same as the one below isn't captured twice.
