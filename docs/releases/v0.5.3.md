Scenecast 0.5.3: Review checks your replica in your own browser, so a screen that matches says it matches, and make a demo from your script.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.5.3** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's new**

- **Make it from my script:** on the Record step, pick your talk track: Scenecast turns it into a plan you check and approve, then records it.
- **Checked in your own browser:** Scenecast draws your replica in Chrome or Edge, the one you recorded in, hidden and with its own empty profile, at the same zoom. Text comes out exactly as it did, so screens that match read as matching.
- **Review shows where a screen differs:** the spot is outlined on both pictures. A changed word, a missing icon or a panel the wrong colour is found even when it's a tiny part of the screen. **Fix it for me** is offered only for those.

**What's fixed**

- The known issue in 0.5.2 (text-heavy screens reading about 1% different) is gone for demos recorded with this version.
- Pictures of each screen are taken without the text cursor, a scrollbar that's fading out, or the pointer resting on a row.
- A zoom level recorded in a tab that was itself zoomed (⌘+ in Chrome) is now the right size.
