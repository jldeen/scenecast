Scenecast 0.4.3: Record works in your real browser, and you see when there's a new version.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.4.3** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's fixed**

- **Record finds the tabs Check just found.** If the recorder was connected from more than one browser (another browser or profile with the extension, or a test browser), Check could open the apps' tabs in one and Record look for them in another: "not open: teams …" right after "Ready to record". Scenecast now records in one browser only, and Check says by name when the recorder is in more than one.
- **Your browser's profiles show** on New demo and the Record step (they stayed on "Looking at the browser's profiles…" when your profiles have pictures).
- No more empty coloured boxes in the sidebar and on New demo.
- The four ways to make a demo are real buttons, for the keyboard and screen readers.

**What's new**

- **A new version shows by itself.** Scenecast checks when it starts, every 30 minutes, and when you come back to its window. A red dot appears on **Check for updates**, with **Update to vX.Y.Z**. Click it to install and restart. Nothing installs on its own, and the button waits while you're recording or presenting.
