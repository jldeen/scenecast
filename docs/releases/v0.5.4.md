Scenecast 0.5.4: Review checks your replica in your own browser, so a screen that matches says it matches, and make a demo from your script.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.5.4** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

(0.5.3 was a test build and was never released.)

**What's new**

- **Make it from my script:** on the Record step, pick your talk track: Scenecast turns it into a plan you check and approve, then records it.
- **Checked in your own browser:** Scenecast draws your replica in Chrome or Edge, the one you recorded in, hidden and with its own empty profile, at the same zoom. Text comes out exactly as it did, so screens that match read as matching.
- **Review shows where a screen differs:** the spot is outlined on both pictures. A changed word, a missing icon or a panel the wrong colour is found even when it's a tiny part of the screen. **Fix it for me** is offered only for those.

**What's fixed**

- **Check for updates works again** after a test update: an address used for testing updates could stay with Scenecast and every check failed.
- **One count everywhere:** the demo list, Present and Review agree on how many screens match; zoom levels and menus that differ are counted on their own.
- **Recordings stay light on a dark Mac,** even when Chrome pauses the recorder part way through a take. Before, the screens after that pause could come out dark.
- An app's address you're typing on the Record step no longer disappears when the recorder connects.
- Review's strip of screens loads small pictures instead of full-size ones.
- The known issue in 0.5.2 (text-heavy screens reading about 1% different) is gone for demos recorded with this version.
- Pictures of each screen are taken without the text cursor, the pointer resting on a row, or the outline a button keeps after you've used it.
- Updating from 0.5.2 works: Scenecast starts with everything it needs, and you stay signed in to Copilot.
- A zoom level recorded in a tab that was itself zoomed (⌘+ in Chrome) is now the right size.

**Known issues**

- On a Mac that always shows scroll bars (with a mouse connected, say), Review can outline a scroll bar as a spot. It's in the recording, not your replica; the next update fixes it.
- If Scenecast's window ever goes blank, quit it (⌘Q) and open it again; nothing is lost.
