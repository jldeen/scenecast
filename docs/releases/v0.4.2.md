Scenecast 0.4.2: you see when there's a new version, without asking.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.4.2** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's new**

- **A new version shows by itself.** Scenecast checks when it starts, every 30 minutes, and when you come back to its window. When there's a newer version, a red dot appears on **Check for updates** and **Update to vX.Y.Z** at the bottom left. Click it to install and restart.
- **Nothing installs on its own,** and the Update button waits while you're recording or presenting.
