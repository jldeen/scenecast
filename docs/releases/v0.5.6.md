Scenecast 0.5.6: Copilot can record your demo in a separate browser, while you do something else.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.5.6** at the bottom left of Scenecast.

**What's new**

- **Copilot records in a separate browser.** On Record, choose **in a separate browser**: Copilot clicks through your plan off your screen, and you keep working. Sign in to it once with **Sign in to it**.
- **Check my script**, before you record: about 7 minutes in the separate browser, changing nothing. It lists where a recording would stop, and what to fix.
- A step Copilot can't prove no longer stops the whole recording when nothing after it changes anything: it marks that beat for you and goes on. It still never makes a change after a step it couldn't prove.
- **Stop** works in a separate browser too, and the demo is put back as it started.

**What's fixed**

- A demo you open from a shared file shows as recorded, keeps its checks, and has its beats, times and guide pictures from the recording it was made with.
- Typing a prompt with ⌥P in a narrow window no longer doubles it.
- Text in Copilot's answers no longer runs into the next message in a narrow window.
- A focus mark no longer lands on empty space when its words are under the message box.
