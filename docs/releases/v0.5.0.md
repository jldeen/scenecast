Scenecast 0.5.0: a new Scenecast. Record, Review, Present, Share, with one next step on every screen, and the app doing the setup for you.

**Update:** click **Update to v0.5.0** at the bottom left of Scenecast. Your demos stay as they are.

**What's new**

- **A new look.** A dark rail with your demos, and four steps along the top of each demo: **Record**, **Review**, **Present**, **Share**. Every screen has one main button: the next thing to do. Folders, ports and other technical details are under **Details**, out of the way.
- **New demo from your open tabs.** Open your demo's apps in your browser, then name the demo and tick them from the list. Microsoft product pages are ticked for you (your mail isn't). The button says what it makes: **Create with 4 apps**. You can still type an address in.
- **Record is your run of show.** Before the show: your browser, the recorder and each app's tab, checked for you ("All set"). If something isn't right, its own button fixes it: Scenecast opens a tab that isn't open, asks **Which tab is it?** if it can't find one, and keeps the new address of an app that moved. Then the show: your talk track's beats, with their times.
- **One check, not two.** **Start recording** checks and fixes, then starts. "Ready to record" followed by "not open" can't happen any more.
- **A small window beside your browser while you record.** It says **Ready: click next** or **Hold on: capturing**, counts the screens, and has **Pause** and **Stop**. If a click comes too early, it says so. When Copilot records, its **May I?** questions show there too.
- **Closing the debugging bar no longer loses your recording.** It pauses, with every screen kept: **Pick up where I left off**.
- **Copilot on any demo.** On Record: **or let Copilot click for you** (say what to show, read its plan, **Approve and start**), or **Coach me** (the next step and what to say while you click). No need to choose when you make the demo.
- **Review makes itself.** When you stop, Scenecast builds the replica, takes out email addresses, names and IDs, puts your talk track on its screens, checks it and compares every screen with the real product. No button. Review then shows each beat with its screens. Pick one to see it next to the real product, and why it differs in a sentence. **Fix it for me** fixes it.
- **Present for the stage.** A ready check first: every screen matches, your guide is made, you've rehearsed, the projector is found. **Rehearse** keeps each beat's time, shown next to your plan. **Present** puts the demo on the projector and your guide on your laptop (**Swap them** if they're the wrong way round). A clicker's **Page Down** moves the stage on.
- **Share one file.** **A Scenecast file**: another presenter double-clicks it and it opens ready to rehearse, with your guide. Or **a folder for any browser**, for someone without Scenecast. Neither has your recording or your sign-ins.

**What's fixed**

- **Your profile, not "Default".** Scenecast records in the browser and profile the recorder is in. It only asks which profile when it can't tell.
- **Dark mode on your computer doesn't stop a recording.** Each app is recorded as it looks.
- **Nothing green unless it worked.** "Recorded: 0 screens" is an error that says why, never a success.
- **Errors in plain words.** What happened, in one sentence, and one button to fix it. The technical words are under **Details**.
- **Delete takes the recording with it**, so no real screenshots are left behind in a hidden folder.
