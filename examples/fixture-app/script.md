# Fixture demo

The sample script for the fixture app (examples/fixture-app/server.mjs): the presenter's lines are the quotes; the
rest is notes. `scenecast script` matches each line to the replica screen it plays over (the expected result is in
packages/selftest/script-focus.mjs).

## Beat 1 · The question

> Hi everyone. Let me show you how we handle a late part.

**Do:** Start on the chat (⌥0).

> I ask about the supplier delay on PO-450101.

**Do:** Click the message box, ⌥P, Enter.

> Kestrel moved the ship date by six days.

> Two builds and three customer orders are at risk, and the earliest is due Friday.

> The Workspace shows what it can use: Work IQ, Fabric IQ and our connectors.

## Beat 2 · Plugins

> Under Plugins, there's Supply chain disruption, the plugin I built.

## Beat 3 · Admin

> In the admin center, IT can search every tool by name.

> Here is alpha-6, published by Fixture.

## Beat 4 · The cost

> Then I ask what it will cost us.

> The cost of the delay is about $35,073, and one action is already approved.

> And our CFO signed off on all of it last quarter.
